I am a Research Fellow at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/mae).

My research lies at the intersection of **digital twins**, **generative artificial intelligence**, and **autonomous manufacturing**. I develop intelligent manufacturing systems that integrate **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Vision Language Models (VLMs)**, **Knowledge Graphs**, and **Reinforcement Learning** to enhance process understanding, decision-making, and human-centric automation.

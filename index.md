I am a Research Fellow at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/mae). I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/), where I also received my Master of Science degree. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Mechatronic Engineering at [Beijing Jiaotong University](https://en.bjtu.edu.cn/).

My research lies at the intersection of **digital twins**, **generative artificial intelligence**, and **autonomous manufacturing**. I develop intelligent manufacturing systems that integrate **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Vision Language Models (VLMs)**, **Knowledge Graphs**, and **Reinforcement Learning** to enhance process understanding, decision-making, and human-centric automation.

I was supervised by [Prof. Jerry Fuh Ying Hsi](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/me/staff/fuh-ying-hsi/) and Prof. Wen Feng Lu from NUS, and [Prof. Bingbing Li](https://www.ecs.csun.edu/~bingbing.li/) from CSUN and UCLA. I have also worked with [Dr. Edward Chow](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/people/edward-chow/) and [Dr. Thomas T. Lu](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/people/thomas-lu/) at NASA JPL. My collaborations span universities and agencies including UCLA, the University of Wisconsin-Madison, Jonkoping University, Cranfield University, and NASA JPL, as well as industry partners including NVIDIA, Autodesk, and Renishaw.

My recent work focuses on LLM/VLM-enabled digital twins, autonomous additive manufacturing, and knowledge-driven human-machine collaboration. I am particularly interested in building manufacturing systems that can reason from multimodal data, adapt to changing process conditions, and support engineers in complex decision-making.
